Help

Connecting

Use Chrome or Edge, with Bluetooth turned on. Turn the toy on (the foot light glows) and keep it close. Press Connect Smart Toy and pick the device whose name starts with ST.

Playing

Pick Hang Out, Team Up or Fight Villains, then click an activity.

Guard Mode

Switch it on and Spidey keeps watch until you switch it off.

Not working?

Turn the toy off and on, close other apps that may be connected to it, and try again. Some activities needed the old Sphero server and may be limited.
